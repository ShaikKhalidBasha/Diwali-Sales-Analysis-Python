🎇 Diwali Sales Analysis using Python

📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Diwali sales data to gain insights into customer behavior, purchasing patterns, and product performance during the festive season.
Using Python libraries, the analysis helps businesses understand which products, categories, and customer segments drive the most sales.

🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics (age, gender, occupation, state)
- Identify top-selling product categories and products
- Understand spending behavior during Diwali
- Provide actionable insights for business decision-making

🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Jupyter Notebook
- Pandas – data manipulation
- NumPy – numerical operations
- Matplotlib & Seaborn – data visualization

🗂️ Dataset

- 11,251 transactions and 15 columns
- 11,239 records after cleaning

📊 Key Analysis Performed

- Data cleaning and preprocessing (dropped blank columns, removed 12 records with missing Amount, fixed data types)
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Gender-wise and age-wise purchasing trends
- State-wise and occupation-wise sales comparison
- Product category and product-level analysis

📌 Key Metrics

Metric - Value 

Total Revenue - ₹10.6 Crore 
Total Purchases - 11,239 
Units Sold - 27,981 
Unique Customers - 3,752 
Average Purchase Value - ₹9,454 

📈 Sample Insights

- Women contribute about 70% of total sales
- Customers aged 26–35 years contribute about 40% of sales
- Top sales come from the IT, Healthcare, and Aviation sectors
- Highest sales are in Uttar Pradesh (18%), Maharashtra (14%), and Karnataka (13%)
- Food (32%), Clothing & Apparel (15.5%), and Electronics & Gadgets (15%) are the top-selling categories

✅ Recommendations

- Target festive campaigns at women aged 26–35 in the top three states
- Stock Food and gifting categories early, since they bring the most revenue
- Focus ad spend on IT, Healthcare, and Aviation professionals
